# Demo script (about 5 minutes; record this as the Round 3 video or present it live)

Before recording: `python3 run.py`, `python3 seed_demo.py`, open `http://127.0.0.1:8000` in a real browser and test it once yourself.

| Time | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Slide: the problem | One line plus a statistic from the plan; guidelines exist but few organisations enforce them, and AI adds volume. |
| 0:30 | Log in as ana, New review, load "Launch email (many issues)" | A creator pastes a draft. Review runs before anything is published. |
| 1:00 | Click highlights, open a claim finding and a terminology finding | Every finding shows evidence, the reason and the guideline or approved fact it comes from. The 99.5% figure contradicts approved fact FACT-UPTIME; the 20% promo uses an expired fact. |
| 1:45 | Accept the "guys" suggestion, show the diff | A suggestion becomes a tracked revision only when the creator accepts. |
| 2:15 | Try to dismiss a high finding as ana | Refused: a reviewer must decide. Humans stay in control. |
| 2:30 | Submit; switch to mira; Request changes with a comment | Reviewer workflow with comments and history. |
| 3:15 | ana fixes and resubmits; mira approves; raj publishes | Revision history, approvals and roles. |
| 3:45 | raj: Insights, Audit log | Repeated mistakes, and rules dismissed often that may mean the guideline is unclear. Everything is audited. |
| 4:30 | AI & data page; show an image item (missing alt text) and a .docx | We disclose AI use: off by default, rules-based. Supports several formats. |
| 4:50 | Slide: limits and roadmap | Be honest: English only, metadata-based visual checks, tested on developer-written cases. |
